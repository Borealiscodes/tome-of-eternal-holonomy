# Runic Magick Codex & Circle of Power Grammar

## 1. Circle of Power Topologies
- **3-Point Triangle:** Base spells (*Alignment + Verb + Noun*).
- **5-Point Pentagon:** Intermediate spells supporting up to **2× Pargon** amplifiers.
- **7-Point Heptagon:** High-potency rituals supporting up to **4× Pargon** amplifiers.
- **9-Point Nonagon:** Grand cosmological bindings capable of stabilizing multi-alignment tension.
